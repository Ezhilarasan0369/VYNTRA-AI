1. Introduction

VYNTRA AI is an AI-powered personal assistant application developed to provide users with assistance for programming, technical questions, IT infrastructure, cloud and networking topics, learning, project development, interview preparation, career guidance, and everyday questions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use VYNTRA AI, how that information is used, and how you can contact us regarding your information.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

When you request access or create an account, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your mobile number
  • Your account password
  • Your account access status

Google Sign-In information

When you use Google Sign-In, VYNTRA AI may receive information made available by Google, such as:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your name
  • Your Google profile picture

3. How We Use Information

Information collected by VYNTRA AI may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process access requests
  • Allow administrators to approve or deny access
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide access to VYNTRA AI features
  • Send account and access-related emails
  • Maintain application security
  • Improve reliability and functionality

4. Google User Data

If you choose to sign in using Google, VYNTRA AI uses the Google account information provided through Google Sign-In for authentication and account management.

Google account information is not used for unrelated advertising purposes and is not sold to third parties.

5. Data Storage

Account information may be stored in the application's backend database for account authentication, access management, and application functionality.

Authentication credentials are processed using security measures designed to protect account information.

6. Email Communications

VYNTRA AI may send transactional emails related to access requests and account status, such as approval or denial notifications.

These emails are intended to support the operation of the VYNTRA AI application.

7. Information Sharing

VYNTRA AI does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application, including authentication, hosting, database, and transactional email services.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect account information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

Account information may be retained while an account is active or as necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and administer the application.

Administrators may permanently delete user accounts and associated access records through the VYNTRA AI administration system.

10. Your Choices

You may request information about your account, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion of your account and associated information.

11. Children's Privacy

VYNTRA AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to VYNTRA AI or its privacy practices.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
